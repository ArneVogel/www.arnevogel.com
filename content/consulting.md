## Reliable LLMs | Distributed Systems | WebAssembly

I am a fourth year PhD candidate doing systems research at [FAU Erlangen-Nürnberg](https://sys.cs.fau.de/en/person/vogel). During this I have done internships at Ericsson (Stockholm) and in the research labs at the Internet Initiative Japan (Tokyo).

My research areas are:
 * Reliable LLMs: how can we make code generated by LLMs trustworthy?
 * Distributed Systems: how can we make theoretical advances usable in practice?
 * WebAssembly: how can we extend the capabilities of WebAssembly to fit our systems?

## Services
 * Research Briefings: Current academic and industry findings on agentic AI reliability for coding, system engineering and formal modelling, WebAssembly and distributed systems
 * System Design Advisory: Review and design of agentic AI architectures with reliability, fault tolerance, and auditability
